# Player Carry

A small Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3 that lets you carry other players.

## Usage

- **Carry:** hold **Shift** and **right-click** a player you are looking at (within reach) with an **empty hand**. They will be mounted on your back.
- **Dismount:** the carried player presses **Shift** to get off.

Only one player can be carried at a time, and a carrier cannot be carried themselves. Dismount placement is handled by vanilla riding mechanics.

## Building

```
./gradlew build
```

The resulting jar is written to `build/libs/`.
